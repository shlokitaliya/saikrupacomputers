How Many CCTV Cameras Does a Shop Need?

Home → Knowledge Center → How Many Cameras

One of the most common questions business owners in Surat ask us is: "How many cameras do I actually need?" Determining the right number of cameras is a balance between covering all your vulnerabilities and managing your budget.

Essential Coverage Areas

For a standard retail shop, you must cover the following critical zones. Each of these zones generally requires at least one dedicated camera:

  • The Main Entrance: You need a high-resolution camera pointed at the door to capture a clear shot of every person who enters or exits the store.
  • The Point of Sale (Cash Register): This is arguably the most important camera indoors. It should monitor the cash drawer and the transaction process clearly to prevent internal theft and resolve customer disputes.
  • The Sales Floor / Aisles: Depending on the size of the shop, you may need one wide-angle dome camera, or several cameras placed at the end of each aisle to monitor customer behavior and deter shoplifting.
  • Stockroom / Inventory Area: The back room where valuable inventory is kept should always be monitored.
  • Back Door / Loading Dock: A common area for break-ins or unauthorized removal of stock.

Calculating the Number

A small shop (like a mobile accessory store or a small bakery) can often be effectively secured with a 4-camera system. This allows for one on the register, one on the door, one overview of the shop, and one in the back.

Medium to large shops (like supermarkets or clothing stores) will typically require an 8-camera or 16-camera system to ensure there are no blind spots behind tall shelving units.
