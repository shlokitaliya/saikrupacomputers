How to Maintain a CCTV Camera System

Home → Knowledge Center → CCTV Maintenance

A CCTV system is not a "set and forget" investment. Like any electronic equipment, it requires regular maintenance. Failing to maintain your cameras could result in blurry footage or complete system failure exactly when you need it most.

Routine Maintenance Checklist

Follow these simple steps every few months to keep your system optimal:

  • Clean the Lenses: Dust, spider webs, and rain spots accumulate on outdoor lenses. Wipe them gently with a microfiber cloth to keep footage sharp and prevent IR reflection at night.
  • Check for Obstructions: Over time, trees or bushes may grow and block a camera's field of view. Trim nearby foliage regularly.
  • Inspect Cables: Look for signs of wear and tear, exposed wires, or water damage on the connections, especially on exterior cameras.
  • Verify Storage: Don't wait until an incident happens to realize your hard drive failed six months ago. Regularly log in to your DVR/NVR and ensure it is actively recording and storing past footage.
  • Update Firmware: Ensure your NVR and IP cameras are running the latest firmware to protect against cybersecurity vulnerabilities.
