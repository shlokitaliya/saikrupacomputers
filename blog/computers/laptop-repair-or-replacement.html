Laptop Repair or Replacement: What Should You Choose?

Home → Knowledge Center → Repair or Replace

When your laptop breaks down or starts running frustratingly slow, you're faced with a difficult decision: should you spend money repairing it, or is it finally time to buy a new one?

At Saikrupa Computers, we help customers in Surat make this decision every day. Here is a practical framework to help you decide.

The 50% Rule

A widely accepted guideline in the tech industry is the 50% rule: If the cost of the repair is more than 50% of the cost of a new laptop with comparable specs, you should usually replace it.

When to REPAIR Your Laptop

  • It's less than 3 years old: Modern laptops should comfortably last 4-6 years. A minor failure (like a dead battery or broken fan) on a 2-year-old laptop is absolutely worth fixing.
  • The upgrade is cheap: If your laptop is slow but structurally fine, upgrading the RAM or swapping the old Hard Drive (HDD) for a Solid State Drive (SSD) is an incredibly cheap repair that breathes new life into the machine.
  • Software Issues: Viruses, corrupt Windows files, or driver conflicts can make a laptop feel dead. A professional format and OS reinstall is very affordable.

When to REPLACE Your Laptop

  • Motherboard Failure on an Old Machine: If the motherboard is dead and the laptop is over 4 years old, the cost of parts and labor often makes repair economically unviable.
  • It can't run modern software: If you're a student or professional who now needs to run heavier software (like video editors or complex 3D software), no amount of repair will make an old, basic processor faster.
  • Severe physical damage: A shattered screen, broken hinges, and a cracked chassis all at once usually point to replacement.
