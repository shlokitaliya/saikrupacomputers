Securing a large facility requires more than simply hanging a few cameras. Our CCTV Project Process

We believe in a methodical approach to security. By carefully planning each step, we deliver reliable surveillance systems that meet your specific operational needs.

  • 1. Understanding Security Requirements: We begin by discussing your primary security concerns, whether it is monitoring inventory, securing perimeters, or overseeing employee safety.
  • 2. Planning Camera Placement: Our team conducts a thorough site survey to identify optimal camera locations, minimizing blind spots and ensuring clear fields of view.
  • 3. Selecting Suitable Equipment: Based on the environment (indoor, outdoor, low-light), we recommend the right mix of cameras, cabling, and storage solutions (DVRs/NVRs).
  • 4. Installation Planning: We schedule the installation to minimize disruption to your daily operations, coordinating wiring and infrastructure needs.
  • 5. System Setup: Our technicians cleanly install all hardware and configure the recording systems for optimal video retention.
  • 6. Testing: Every camera, recording setting, and remote viewing application is rigorously tested before handover.
  • 7. Service and Maintenance: Post-installation, we provide ongoing support to ensure your system remains operational year-round.
